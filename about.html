About Us

Last updated October 17, 2026

BestDealHunter is a deal-discovery website and Instagram account (@best_dealhunter). We look for big discounts, usually 50% off or more, on products from well-known US retailers and marketplaces, mostly eBay today, and also stores such as Best Buy, Macy's and Amazon, and list them in one place so you spend less time searching.

What we do

  • Find deals in retailers' official data. Most of our deals come from eBay's official Browse API, which reports each listing's current price, its original or list price, its condition and its seller. We list only new, Buy It Now items from established sellers, shipping in the US, at 50% off or more.
  • Add deals checked by a person. Some deals are found by our team, who look at the retailer's own product page and save a screenshot of the price, the reference price and the stock before the deal is listed.
  • Label older prices. Each deal shows when it was posted. A deal whose price has not been re-checked in the last 2 days is labelled "Price not re-verified", and deals that are no longer current move to Past Deals, clearly labelled.
  • Link directly to the retailer. We do not sell products, take orders or handle payments; every purchase is made on the retailer's own website.
Prices and availability change. Retailers can change a price, end a sale or sell out at any time, sometimes within minutes. A deal shown here matched the retailer's information when it was listed, but always confirm the final price, shipping, seller and stock on the retailer's website before you buy.

How we make money

BestDealHunter is free to use. Some links on this site and on our Instagram posts are affiliate links. If you click one and make a qualifying purchase, we may earn a commission from the retailer or affiliate network, at no additional cost to you. Affiliate programs we take part in include the eBay Partner Network and the Amazon Associates program. As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

Commissions never decide whether a deal is listed: a deal must meet the same discount and verification rules whether or not the link earns a commission.

Product images

Deal images are product photos from the listing or illustrative graphics. Some graphics are made with design or AI image tools to show the type of product; they are not evidence of a price, and the retailer's page is always the final word on what you will receive.
