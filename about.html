About Us

Last updated October 16, 2026

BestDealHunter is a deal-discovery website and Instagram account (@best_dealhunter). We look for big discounts, usually 50% off or more, on products sold by well-known US retailers such as Amazon, eBay, Walmart, Target, Best Buy, Home Depot, Lowe's, Macy's and Dick's Sporting Goods, and list them in one place so you spend less time searching.

What we do

  • Collect deals from retailers' official product feeds and APIs (for example, the eBay Browse API) and from deals submitted by our team.
  • Check each deal against the retailer's own product page before it is listed: the current price, the original or reference price, the discount, that the item is in stock, and who sells it.
  • Show the date and time each deal was last checked. Deals that are no longer current move to Past Deals and are clearly labelled.
  • Link directly to the retailer. We do not sell products, take orders or handle payments; every purchase is made on the retailer's own website.
Prices and availability change. Retailers can change a price, end a sale or sell out at any time, sometimes within minutes. A deal shown here was accurate when we checked it, but always confirm the final price, shipping, seller and stock on the retailer's website before you buy.

How we make money

BestDealHunter is free to use. Some links on this site and on our Instagram posts are affiliate links. If you click one and make a qualifying purchase, we may earn a commission from the retailer or affiliate network, at no additional cost to you. Affiliate programs we take part in include the eBay Partner Network and the Amazon Associates program. As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

Commissions never decide whether a deal is listed: a deal must meet the same discount and verification rules whether or not the link earns a commission.

Product images

Deal images are product photos or illustrative graphics. Some graphics are made with design or AI image tools to show the type of product; they are not evidence of a price, and the retailer's page is always the final word on what you will receive.
